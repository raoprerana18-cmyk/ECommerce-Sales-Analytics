# Outlier Analysis – Findings

**Project:** E-Commerce Sales, Customer Churn & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## Method
The Interquartile Range (IQR) method was used to identify potential outliers.

## Key Findings
1. Orders: 773 potential outliers in order_total_usd.
2. Delivery days: No potential outliers.
3. Order item quantity: 931 potential outliers.
4. Line total: 2,671 potential outliers.
5. Line profit: 1,979 potential outliers.
6. Product list price: 33 potential outliers.
7. Product unit cost: 42 potential outliers.
8. Product weight: No potential outliers.

## Investigation
The 10 highest orders were inspected. They represent high-value, multi-item orders across different countries. All 10 were marked as Delivered.

## Conclusion
Potential outliers were retained because they may represent genuine business transactions. No values were deleted or modified solely because they exceeded the IQR limits.
