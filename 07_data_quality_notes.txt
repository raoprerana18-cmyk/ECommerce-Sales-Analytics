# Data Quality Checks – Final Findings

**Project:** E-Commerce Sales, Customer Churn & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Basic Validation
- No zero or negative order totals found.
- No zero or negative exchange rates found.
- No negative item counts found.
- No zero or negative quantities found.
- No negative line totals found.
- No invalid product prices, costs or weights found.

## 2. Negative Profit Investigation
- 484 order-item records have negative profit.
- These records were retained because they may represent genuine loss-making transactions.

## 3. Profit Calculation Validation

The verified formula is:

Profit = Line Revenue − (Unit Cost × Quantity)

- Total records checked: 29,671.
- Records matching within $0.01 tolerance: 29,671.
- Records differing by $0.01: 2.

The two small differences may be related to rounding. Their original values were retained.

## 4. Conclusion

The datasets passed the initial data quality checks. The profit calculation was validated within a $0.01 tolerance. No original data was deleted or modified during this investigation.
### Profit Calculation Validation

During data quality validation, the initial profit calculation was found to be inconsistent because it did not account for item quantity.

After testing an alternative formula, the following calculation matched 29,669 of the 29,671 order-item records within a tolerance of $0.01:

**Profit = Line Revenue − (Unit Cost × Quantity)**

Only two records exceeded the tolerance and require further investigation.

The original profit values have been retained without modification.
